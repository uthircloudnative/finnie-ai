# India Income Tax & Investment Rules (Assessment Year 2026-27)

This document contains the official Indian tax deduction limits and capital gains tax rates for the financial year 2025-26 (Assessment Year 2026-27).

## 1. Section 80C Deductions (Old Tax Regime)
The total aggregate deduction for various investments and expenses is **₹1.5 Lakh**.
- **Eligible Investments**: Public Provident Fund (PPF), Equity Linked Savings Scheme (ELSS), Employees' Provident Fund (EPF), Life Insurance Premium, NSC.
- **Tuition Fees**: For two children (included in ₹1.5L limit).
- **Home Loan Principal**: Included in ₹1.5L limit.

## 2. National Pension System (NPS) Deductions
- **Section 80CCD(1)**: Self-contributions (Part of the total ₹1.5L 80C limit).
- **Section 80CCD(1B)**: Additional deduction of **₹50,000** for self-contributions. (Total potential deduction = ₹2 Lakh).
- **Section 80CCD(2)**: Employer contributions (Up to 10% of basic salary + DA, 14% for government employees).

## 3. Capital Gains Tax on Mutual Funds (Post-Budget 2024 update)
For equity-oriented mutual funds (more than 65% equity exposure):
- **Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG)**: **20%** (if held for less than 12 months).
- **Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG)**: **12.5%** (if held for more than 12 months).
- **LTCG Exemption**: Up to **₹1.25 Lakh** per financial year is exempt.

## 4. Other Key Deductions
- **Section 80D (Health Insurance)**: ₹25,000 (Self/Family) + ₹50,000 (Senior Citizen Parents).
- **Standard Deduction**: ₹75,000 (Updated for salaries in New Tax Regime).

## 5. Lock-in Periods
- **PPF**: 15 years (Partial withdrawals allowed from the 7th year).
- **ELSS**: 3 years (Shortest lock-in for tax-saving).
- **NPS**: Up to age 60 (With partial withdrawal for specified reasons).
